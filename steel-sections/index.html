Steel sections

Dimensions, weights and drawings of European and British steel profiles

A free reference of the hot-rolled and hollow steel sections in the Steel Profiles catalog: every size with its dimensions, mass per metre and cross-sectional area, and a scale drawing for each hot-rolled section. The data is the same the app ships with.

The app adds the structural properties (moments of inertia, section moduli, radii of gyration, torsional and warping constants, Eurocode 3 classes), the American AISC 14 and 16, Canadian CISC and Australian AS/NZS libraries, design checks to Eurocode 3 and AISC 360, and tools to find equivalent sections across standards.
