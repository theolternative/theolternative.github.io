Universal beams (UB)

British I beams for bending · 81 sizes · ASTM A6/A6M-07, BS 4-1:2005

Universal beams to BS 4-1:2005 are the British I beams with parallel flanges, designated by nominal depth, nominal width and mass per metre (203 x 133 x 25 UB). Depth is much greater than width, which makes them efficient in bending.

The table lists the dimensions, mass and area of every size. Tap a designation for its drawing and detailed dimensions. All values are in millimetres, kilograms per metre and square centimetres.
