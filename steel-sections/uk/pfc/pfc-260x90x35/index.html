PFC 260 x 90 x 35

260 x 90 x 35 PFC · British channels with parallel flanges · BS 4-1:2005

PFC 260 x 90 x 35 is one of the Parallel flange channels (PFC) of the British library (british channels with parallel flanges): 260 mm deep and 90 mm wide, web 8 mm, flanges 14 mm, 34.8 kg/m, area 44.38 cm².
