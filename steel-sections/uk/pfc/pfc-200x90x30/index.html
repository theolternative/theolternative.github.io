PFC 200 x 90 x 30

200 x 90 x 30 PFC · British channels with parallel flanges · BS 4-1:2005

PFC 200 x 90 x 30 is one of the Parallel flange channels (PFC) of the British library (british channels with parallel flanges): 200 mm deep and 90 mm wide, web 7 mm, flanges 14 mm, 29.7 kg/m, area 37.86 cm².
