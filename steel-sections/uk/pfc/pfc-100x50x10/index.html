PFC 100 x 50 x 10

100 x 50 x 10 PFC · British channels with parallel flanges · BS 4-1:2005

PFC 100 x 50 x 10 is one of the Parallel flange channels (PFC) of the British library (british channels with parallel flanges): 100 mm deep and 50 mm wide, web 5 mm, flanges 8.5 mm, 10.2 kg/m, area 13 cm².
