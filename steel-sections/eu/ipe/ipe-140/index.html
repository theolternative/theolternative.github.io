IPE 140

European I beams with parallel flanges · Euronorm 19-57

IPE 140 is one of the IPE beams of the European library (european i beams with parallel flanges): 140 mm deep and 73 mm wide, web 4.7 mm, flanges 6.9 mm, 12.9 kg/m, area 16.4 cm².
