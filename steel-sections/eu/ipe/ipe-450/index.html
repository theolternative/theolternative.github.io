IPE 450

European I beams with parallel flanges · Euronorm 19-57

IPE 450 is one of the IPE beams of the European library (european i beams with parallel flanges): 450 mm deep and 190 mm wide, web 9.4 mm, flanges 14.6 mm, 77.6 kg/m, area 98.8 cm².
