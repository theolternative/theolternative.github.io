Bulb flats (Holland profiles, EN 10067)

Hot-rolled bulb flats for shipbuilding · 68 sizes · EN 10067

Bulb flats to EN 10067, also called Holland profiles or HP flats, are the stiffeners of the shipbuilding industry: a flat bar with a bulb on one edge, welded to plating as deck, side and bottom stiffeners. The designation gives the depth and the web thickness in millimetres, from HP 60 x 4 to HP 430 x 20.

The table lists the dimensions, mass and area of every size. Tap a designation for its drawing and detailed dimensions. All values are in millimetres, kilograms per metre and square centimetres.
