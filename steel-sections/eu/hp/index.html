HP bearing piles

Wide flange bearing piles · 31 sizes · mill catalogue

HP bearing piles are wide flange sections with equal flange and web thickness, rolled for driven piling. Not to be confused with the HP bulb flats (Holland profiles) used in shipbuilding, which are listed under bulb flats.

The table lists the dimensions, mass and area of every size. Tap a designation for its drawing and detailed dimensions. All values are in millimetres, kilograms per metre and square centimetres.
