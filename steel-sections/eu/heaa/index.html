HEAA sections

European wide flange beams, extra-light series · 24 sizes · ArcelorMittal sections catalogue

HEAA (HE AA) sections are the lightest of the European wide flange series: the same width as HEA, HEB and HEM of the same nominal size but thinner flanges and web. They are rolled by ArcelorMittal.

The table lists the dimensions, mass and area of every size. Tap a designation for its drawing and detailed dimensions. All values are in millimetres, kilograms per metre and square centimetres.
