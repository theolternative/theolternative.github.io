HD sections

Wide flange columns · 38 sizes · ASTM A6/A6M-07, ArcelorMittal sections catalogue

HD sections are the heavy column sections of the ArcelorMittal range: 260 to 400 mm nominal depth with masses up to 1,299 kg/m, used for columns in tall buildings and heavily loaded structures.
