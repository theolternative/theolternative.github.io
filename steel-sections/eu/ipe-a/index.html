IPE A beams

Light IPE variants · 18 sizes · ArcelorMittal sections catalogue

IPE A beams are the lightweight version of the IPE range: same nominal depth, thinner web and flanges, lower mass per metre. They are rolled by ArcelorMittal and listed in its sections catalogue.

The table lists the dimensions, mass and area of every size. Tap a designation for its drawing and detailed dimensions. All values are in millimetres, kilograms per metre and square centimetres.
