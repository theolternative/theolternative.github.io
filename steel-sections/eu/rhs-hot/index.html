RHS hot-finished (EN 10210)

Rectangular hollow sections, hot-finished · 163 sizes · EN 10210

Hot-finished rectangular hollow sections to EN 10210. The designation gives width, depth and wall thickness in millimetres. Hot-finished sections have smaller corner radii and no residual stresses from forming, which gives them a better buckling curve than cold-formed sections in Eurocode 3.

The table lists the dimensions, mass and area of every size. All values are in millimetres, kilograms per metre and square centimetres.
