IPN beams

European I beams with tapered flanges · 21 sizes · DIN 1025-1:1963, DIN 1025-1:1995 and NF A 45-209:1983

IPN beams (DIN 1025-1, formerly INP) are the narrow flange I beams with 14 % tapered inner flange faces. They predate the IPE range and are still produced for replacement work, crane runways and small spans. The number is the nominal depth in millimetres.

The table lists the dimensions, mass and area of every size. Tap a designation for its drawing and detailed dimensions. All values are in millimetres, kilograms per metre and square centimetres.
