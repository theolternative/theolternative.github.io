IPN 100

European I beams with tapered flanges · DIN 1025-1:1995 and NF A 45-209:1983

IPN 100 is one of the IPN beams of the European library (european i beams with tapered flanges): 100 mm deep and 50 mm wide, web 4.5 mm, flanges 6.8 mm, 8.34 kg/m, area 10.6 cm².
