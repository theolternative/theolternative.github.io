IPN 260

European I beams with tapered flanges · DIN 1025-1:1995 and NF A 45-209:1983

IPN 260 is one of the IPN beams of the European library (european i beams with tapered flanges): 260 mm deep and 113 mm wide, web 9.4 mm, flanges 14.1 mm, 41.9 kg/m, area 53.3 cm².
