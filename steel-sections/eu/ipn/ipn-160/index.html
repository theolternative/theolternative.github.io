IPN 160

European I beams with tapered flanges · DIN 1025-1:1995 and NF A 45-209:1983

IPN 160 is one of the IPN beams of the European library (european i beams with tapered flanges): 160 mm deep and 74 mm wide, web 6.3 mm, flanges 9.5 mm, 17.9 kg/m, area 22.8 cm².
