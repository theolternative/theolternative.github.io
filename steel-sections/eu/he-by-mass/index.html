HE sections by mass

Wide flange columns designated by mass · 16 sizes · ASTM A6/A6M-07, Euronorm 53-62

These HE sections are designated by depth and mass per metre (for example HE 650 x 343) instead of the A/B/M letters. They are the very heavy column sections of the ArcelorMittal range, rolled to ASTM A6/A6M profiles with the dimensions of the HD/HL families.

The table lists the dimensions, mass and area of every size. Tap a designation for its drawing and detailed dimensions. All values are in millimetres, kilograms per metre and square centimetres.
