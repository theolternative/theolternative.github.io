UPN 350

European channels with tapered flanges · DIN 1026-1:2000 and NF A 45-202:1986

UPN 350 is one of the UPN channels of the European library (european channels with tapered flanges): 350 mm deep and 100 mm wide, web 14 mm, flanges 16 mm, 60.6 kg/m, area 77.3 cm².
