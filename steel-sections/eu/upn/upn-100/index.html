UPN 100

European channels with tapered flanges · DIN 1026-1:2000 and NF A 45-202:1986

UPN 100 is one of the UPN channels of the European library (european channels with tapered flanges): 100 mm deep and 50 mm wide, web 6 mm, flanges 8.5 mm, 10.6 kg/m, area 13.5 cm².
