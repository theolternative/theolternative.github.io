UPN 280

European channels with tapered flanges · DIN 1026-1:2000 and NF A 45-202:1986

UPN 280 is one of the UPN channels of the European library (european channels with tapered flanges): 280 mm deep and 95 mm wide, web 10 mm, flanges 15 mm, 41.8 kg/m, area 53.3 cm².
