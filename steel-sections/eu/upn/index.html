UPN channels

European channels with tapered flanges · 18 sizes · DIN 1026-1:2000 and NF A 45-202:1986

UPN channels (DIN 1026-1, formerly U or UNP) have 8 % tapered inner flange faces. The number is the depth in millimetres, from UPN 80 to UPN 400. They are used for purlins, trimmers, bracing and in pairs as built-up columns.

The table lists the dimensions, mass and area of every size. Tap a designation for its drawing and detailed dimensions. All values are in millimetres, kilograms per metre and square centimetres.
