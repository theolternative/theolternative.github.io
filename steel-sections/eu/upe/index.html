UPE channels

European channels with parallel flanges · 14 sizes · DIN 1026-2:2002-10

UPE channels (DIN 1026-2) are the European parallel flange channels. The number is the depth in millimetres. Compared with UPN of the same depth they have a wider flange and a parallel inner face, which makes bolting easier.

The table lists the dimensions, mass and area of every size. Tap a designation for its drawing and detailed dimensions. All values are in millimetres, kilograms per metre and square centimetres.
