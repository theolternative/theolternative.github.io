UPE 180

European channels with parallel flanges · DIN 1026-2:2002-10

UPE 180 is one of the UPE channels of the European library (european channels with parallel flanges): 180 mm deep and 75 mm wide, web 5.5 mm, flanges 10.5 mm, 19.7 kg/m, area 25.1 cm².
