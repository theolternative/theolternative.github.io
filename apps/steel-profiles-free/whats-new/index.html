What's New in Steel Profiles

Release notes for iPhone, iPad and Mac

6.0.3

October 2026 · iOS, iPadOS

  • Free previews: see every property of any section without buying its library, up to three sections a day. A previewed section stays open until midnight; export, design checks and equivalents still follow your purchases.
  • Unlock a library in one tap from the profile page, with the price on the button. The purchase sheet lists what a library adds and says it is a one-time purchase.
  • On iPhones and iPads managed by a company or school, where in-app purchases are turned off, the app explains why and points to Steel Profiles Pro for teams and schools, with a request you can send to your IT team.

6.0.2

October 2026 · iOS, iPadOS · also Steel Profiles Pro 6.0.2

  • Fixed: opening a comparison from the Equivalent Finder results could freeze the app on iOS 17 and later.
  • The app opens the Tools tab from a link: steelprofiles://tools/designChecks (Pro: steelprofilespro://).

6.0.1

October 2026 · iOS, iPadOS

  • Minor improvements and bug fixes.
  • Steel Profiles AISC is renamed Steel Profiles Pro and becomes the complete edition for companies and schools: every library and the design checks, no in-app purchases. Existing owners get it as a free update.

6.0

October 2026 · iOS, iPadOS, macOS

  • Mac app: Steel Profiles runs natively on macOS 13 and later, with a sidebar, keyboard navigation and the same tools as iPhone and iPad.
  • iCloud sync: favorites, projects, weight lists and saved sections stay up to date on every device, including between the free app and Steel Profiles Pro.
  • Design Checks: EN 1993-1-1 (Eurocode 3) and AISC 360-22 resistances for axial force, bending and shear, flexural and lateral-torsional buckling and beam-column interaction (EC3 Annex B method 2, AISC H1 with Appendix 8 amplification), with every formula and intermediate value shown.
  • Section Builder: properties of welded and built-up sections from plates and tubes, with presets for welded I and box sections, built-up channels, tees, angles and plated beams.
  • Bolts: metric M8 to M64 to EN 1993-1-8 and imperial 1/2" to 1 1/2" to AISC 360, with shear, tension, bearing, preload and slip resistance.
  • Steel grades: EN 10025, EN 10210/10219, ASTM, CSA, AS/NZS and JIS strengths by thickness, with approximate equivalents.
  • Bulb flats (Holland profiles, EN 10067): 68 sizes from HP 60 x 4 to HP 430 x 20 in the European library, after a request from a ship structures specialist.
  • Export: profiles and comparisons as PDF or CSV, projects as CSV.
  • Equivalent Finder matches within the same shape family and shows clear source and target libraries; Comparator picks sections from any library.
  • Search a series, such as "HEA", to list all its sizes; "HEA 200", "HEA200" and "HE 200 A" all find the same section.
  • Imperial dimensions can be shown as fractions to 1/16".
  • Much faster start-up on the Mac with large libraries.
  • Fixed: the app could fail to open after restoring an iPhone from a backup.
  • Many other improvements and fixes. Purchases made in earlier versions remain valid; use Restore Purchases after a reinstall.

Get the update

Steel Profiles is free on the App Store with in-app purchases for the libraries. Companies and schools buying through Apple Business or Apple School Manager can use Steel Profiles Pro (how to buy for a team).
