Steel Profiles

Sections, design checks, bolts

Description

Steel Profiles is the steel section reference for structural engineers, fabricators, detailers and contractors. Look up nearly 10,000 sections from six international standards, check them to Eurocode 3 or AISC 360, and keep your take-offs in sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Every section comes with its complete geometric and structural properties, a description of each value and a cross-section drawing. Version 6.0 adds design checks, a section builder, bolt and steel grade references, bulb flats, iCloud sync and a native Mac app (release notes).

Section Properties

  • Nearly 10,000 sections from six international standards (AISC 16 and 14, CISC, European, British, Australian/NZ)
  • Complete geometric and structural properties with descriptions and a cross-section drawing
  • Filters by area, weight, moment of inertia, section moduli and radii of gyration
  • Smart search: HEA 200, HEA200 and W10x49 all find their section; a series name lists every size
  • Favorites, Spotlight search and Siri
  • Copy, PDF and CSV export of properties and comparisons; optional imperial fractions
  • Works offline — no internet connection required

Design Checks

  • EN 1993-1-1 (Eurocode 3) and AISC 360-22, with every formula and intermediate value shown
  • Cross-section classification, axial force, bending, shear
  • Flexural buckling and lateral-torsional buckling
  • Beam-column interaction (EC3 Annex B method 2, AISC H1 with Appendix 8 amplification)
  • Steel grade selected by code family, strengths by thickness
  • Results are design aids for preliminary sizing and must be verified by a qualified engineer

Tools

  • Equivalent Finder: the closest section in another standard, optionally from the same shape family
  • Comparator: up to three sections side by side, each in its own units
  • Section Builder: welded I and box sections, built-up channels, tees and angles, plated beams, with full properties
  • Bolts: metric M8 to M64 and imperial 1/2" to 1 1/2", with shear, tension, bearing and slip resistance
  • Steel grades: EN 10025, EN 10210/10219, ASTM, CSA, AS/NZS and JIS, with approximate equivalents
  • Weight lists organized in projects and folders, exported to CSV
  • Unit converter for engineering units
  • iCloud sync of favorites, projects and saved sections across all your devices

Libraries

  • USA: AISC 16 and AISC 16 SI (16th Edition), AISC 14 and AISC 14 SI (14th Edition)
  • Canada: CISC 92
  • Europe: IPE, HE (HEA, HEB, HEM, HEAA), HD, HL, HP, IPN, UPE, UPN, angles, tees, bulb flats (EN 10067), hot-finished and cold-formed hollow sections
  • United Kingdom: UB, UC, UBP, J, PFC, T, angles, hollow sections
  • Australia / New Zealand: AS/NZS
