Steel Profiles Free

Multi-Standard Steel Section Database

Description

Steel Profiles Free is a comprehensive steel section reference tool covering major international standards — American (AISC), European (Euronorm), British (BS), Canadian (CISC), and Australian/New Zealand (AS/NZS). Start with a free selection of profiles and unlock additional databases as you need them.

Available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Every profile includes full geometry properties with detailed descriptions and cross-section graphics. Search by name or filter by area, weight, moment of inertia, and more. Rotate your device to maximize the data on screen, and switch between dark and light themes to suit your environment.

Features

  • Steel section databases from 5 international standards (American, European, British, Canadian, Australian/NZ)
  • Full geometry properties with detailed descriptions for every profile
  • Search shapes by name or filter by area, weight, moment of inertia, elastic modulus, and radius of gyration
  • Detailed cross-section graphics enhanced for Retina displays
  • iPad Table view for comparing multiple shapes and properties side by side
  • iPad All-in-one view showing shapes and selected properties together
  • Landscape orientation for maximum data visibility
  • Dark and light theme
  • Works offline — no internet connection required
  • Unlock additional standards via in-app purchases
