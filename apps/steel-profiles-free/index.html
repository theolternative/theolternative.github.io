Steel Profiles

Steel Section Properties

Description

Steel Profiles is a comprehensive reference tool for structural engineers and construction professionals, covering over 5,000 steel sections from six international standards — American (AISC), European (Euronorm), British (BS), Canadian (CISC), and Australian/New Zealand (AS/NZS). Start with a free selection of profiles and unlock additional databases as you need them.

Available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision. Browse and search by name or properties, compare up to 3 profiles side by side, find equivalent sections across different standards, and calculate weights organized by project. Every profile includes full geometry properties with detailed descriptions and cross-section drawings.

Features

  • Over 5,000 steel sections from 6 international standards (American AISC 14 & 16, European, British, Canadian, Australian/NZ)
  • Compare up to 3 profiles simultaneously
  • Equivalent section finder across different standards
  • Weight calculator with project organization
  • Full geometry and structural properties with detailed descriptions
  • Detailed cross-section technical drawings
  • Advanced filtering by area, weight, moment of inertia, elastic modulus, and more
  • Favorites for quick access to frequently used profiles
  • Spotlight search and Siri Shortcuts integration
  • Dark mode support
  • Works offline — no internet connection required
  • Unlock additional standards via in-app purchases
