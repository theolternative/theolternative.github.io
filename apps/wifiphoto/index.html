WiFiPhoto

Wireless Photo & Video Transfer

Description

WiFiPhoto is the fastest way to download full quality photos and videos from your Camera Roll or Photo Library to any computer over a Wi-Fi network. No dock, no USB cable, no compromise on quality — just pick your photos or videos and point your browser to the web address displayed to instantly download a zipped archive with all your picks.

Available on iPhone and iPad since 2009, WiFiPhoto has been refined over the years to deliver a simple, reliable wireless transfer experience. Streaming transfers mean downloads start instantly with no wait time, and auto-reconnect ensures the server restarts when your Wi-Fi reconnects.

Features

  • Transfer full quality photos and videos over Wi-Fi — no cables needed
  • Streaming transfers: downloads start instantly with no wait time
  • Auto-reconnect: server auto-restarts when Wi-Fi reconnects
  • Download as a zipped archive from any web browser
  • Custom filenames with placeholders
  • iPad drag & drop support
  • Select by date with "Select all" for each group
  • Haptic feedback on selection and bulk actions
  • Full VoiceOver accessibility
  • Optimized memory management for large photo libraries
