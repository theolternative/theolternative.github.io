WiFiPhoto

Photo Transfer Toolkit

Description

WiFiPhoto is a comprehensive image processing and batch export toolkit that goes beyond simple photo transfers. Build custom templates to resize, convert formats, add timestamps or watermarks, then process hundreds of photos simultaneously. Share results via Wi-Fi to any browser, AirDrop, Files, Mail, or save directly to the Photo Library.

Create export templates with format conversion (JPEG/PNG), resizing, timestamp and watermark overlays, then batch-process your selections with a live preview before committing. On the receiving end, scan a QR code or type a URL on your computer to download files as a zip or browse a web gallery where you can pick specific photos.
