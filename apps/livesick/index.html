Livesick

Create playlists from setlists

Description

Relive every song of your favourite music events. Search for artists, browse their concert setlists, and instantly create Apple Music playlists ready to play — so you can experience the show again, track by track.

Whether you just got back from a concert or want to revisit a legendary performance, Livesick matches each setlist entry to songs on Apple Music. Save your favourite events, play songs directly in the app, or export full playlists to your Apple Music library.

Features

  • Search for any artist and browse their concert history
  • Download setlists from past and upcoming events
  • Automatically match setlist songs to Apple Music tracks
  • Play songs directly within the app
  • Create and save Apple Music playlists from setlists
  • Save favourite events for quick access
  • Free to use — requires an active Apple Music subscription

Compatibility

iOS 16.0+ iPhone iPad Apple Vision Pro

Languages
