Steel Profiles AISC

AISC 14 Steel Section Database

Description

Steel Profiles AISC is a full-featured reference tool for structural engineers working with American steel standards. Access the complete AISC 14 steel shapes database in both US Customary and SI units, with geometry, detailing, and design properties for every shape.

Look up any profile instantly, sort by properties, and convert values to any unit you need. Available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac — whether you're sizing beams at your desk or checking sections on-site, the data is always at hand, no internet connection required.

Features

  • Complete AISC 14 & AISC 14 SI steel section databases
  • All shape types: W, S, M, HP, C, MC, L, WT, ST, MT, HSS (Round, Rectangular), 2L, and Pipe
  • Full set of properties: area, weight, moments of inertia, section modulus, radius of gyration, and more
  • Sort shapes by any property in ascending or descending order
  • Search by name or filter by minimum area, weight, moment of inertia, elastic modulus, and radius of gyration
  • Convert all values to any unit (mm, cm, m, in)
  • Detailed cross-section graphics enhanced for Retina displays
  • iPad Table view for comparing multiple shapes side by side
  • Dark and light theme
  • Works offline — no internet connection required
