Description

Steel Profiles Pro is the complete edition of Steel Profiles: every library, the full properties of every section and the design checks, in a single paid app with no in-app purchases.

It is the edition for companies and schools. Buy it in volume through Apple Business Manager or Apple School Manager and deploy it to managed iPhones and iPads, where in-app purchases are not available. Individual users get the same features in the free Steel Profiles app with the All Libraries purchase.

Steel Profiles Pro is the new name of Steel Profiles AISC. Existing owners receive the complete app as a free update. See what's new in 6.0, and the free steel section tables and guides.

For Companies and Schools

  • Volume purchase through Apple Business Manager and Apple School Manager, with managed distribution to devices or users
  • Nothing to unlock on each device: all libraries and design checks are included
  • Educational institutions buying 20 or more copies through Apple School Manager get a 50% volume discount
  • Favorites, projects and saved sections sync through iCloud, also with the free Steel Profiles app on the same Apple ID
  • Works offline on site, in the shop and in the classroom

Everything Included

  • Nearly 10,000 sections with complete properties: AISC 16 and 14 (US and SI), CISC 92, European, British, Australian/NZ
  • Design checks to EN 1993-1-1 and AISC 360-22: classification, axial force, bending, shear, flexural and lateral-torsional buckling, beam-column interaction, with every formula shown
  • Equivalent Finder across standards and Comparator for up to three sections
  • Section Builder for welded and built-up sections
  • Bolts M8 to M64 and 1/2" to 1 1/2" with shear, tension, bearing and slip resistance
  • Steel grades to EN, ASTM, CSA, AS/NZS and JIS, with strengths by thickness
  • Weight lists in projects and folders, PDF and CSV export, unit converter
  • Filters, smart search, favorites, Spotlight and Siri
  • Results are design aids for preliminary sizing and must be verified by a qualified engineer
