Questions from IT

Can a licence move to someone else?

Yes. Licences bought in Apple Business or Apple School Manager belong to the organisation. Take one back from a person or a device in your device management and assign it again.

Does each person need an Apple Account?

No, if you assign the copies to devices. If you assign them to people, they sign in with a Managed Apple Account or their own. iCloud sync of favorites and projects needs an Apple Account on the device.

What data does the app collect?

None. There is no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Favorites and weight lists stay on the device, or in the user's own iCloud when sync is on. See the privacy policy.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. All 9,859 sections, the design checks and the references are inside the 32 MB app, so it works on site, in the shop and in the classroom without a network.

Is there a Mac version?

Steel Profiles Pro is for iPhone and iPad. On a Mac, use the free Steel Profiles with the All Libraries purchase, bought per person with an Apple Account on a Mac where purchases are allowed.

We already deployed the free app. What changes?

Deploy Pro next to it and remove the free app once people have moved over. Favorites, projects and saved sections carry over through iCloud when both apps use the same Apple Account. In-app purchases made in the free app do not transfer to Pro.

Is there a discount for companies?

Apple gives volume discounts to education only, so companies pay the App Store price for each copy. For a large order, or a question before you buy, write to support@theolternative.com.

Are the design checks certified?

No. They follow EN 1993-1-1 and AISC 360-22 and show every formula, but they are aids for preliminary sizing and a qualified engineer must verify the results.

Which languages does it speak?

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese and Simplified Chinese. It follows the language of the device.
