Bolt resistances to Eurocode 3

Shear, tension and preload of M12 to M36 bolts in classes 8.8 and 10.9, EN 1993-1-8 Table 3.4

EN 1993-1-8 gives the design resistance of a single bolt in a bearing-type connection with three formulas and one partial factor, γM2 = 1.25 (recommended value, confirmed by most National Annexes). The values below are computed from them for the common structural sizes and classes; for the bearing resistance, which depends on the plate and the bolt spacing, and for slip-resistant connections, use the Bolts tool in Steel Profiles.

Formulas

  • Shear per shear plane: Fv,Rd = αv fub A / γM2. With the threads in the shear plane A is the tensile stress area As and αv = 0.6 for classes 4.6, 5.6 and 8.8, 0.5 for class 10.9. With the shank in the shear plane A is the gross area and αv = 0.6 for every class.
  • Tension: Ft,Rd = k2 fub As / γM2, with k2 = 0.9 (0.63 for countersunk bolts).
  • Combined shear and tension: Fv,Ed / Fv,Rd + Ft,Ed / (1.4 Ft,Rd) ≤ 1.0.
  • Preload (classes 8.8 and 10.9 only, EN 14399 assemblies): Fp,C = 0.7 fub As. The slip resistance per bolt is then Fs,Rd = ks n μ Fp,C / γM3 with γM3 = 1.25 at ultimate (1.1 at serviceability), μ = 0.5 for class A surfaces down to 0.2 for class D.

fub is 800 MPa for class 8.8 and 1,000 MPa for class 10.9 (400 and 500 MPa for 4.6 and 5.6). The bolt must also be checked for bearing on the plate: Fb,Rd = k1 αb fu d t / γM2, which usually governs with thin plates or small end distances.

With the shank in the shear plane the shear resistance rises to 0.6 fub A / γM2 on the gross area: 120.6 kN for an M20 8.8 and 150.8 kN for an M20 10.9. Note that a 10.9 bolt gains little in shear through the threads over an 8.8 (αv drops to 0.5) but 25 % in tension and preload.

Reading the table

  • A double shear connection (bolt through three plates) has two shear planes: multiply Fv,Rd by 2.
  • The resistances are design values: compare them with factored actions (FEd), not with working loads.
  • Minimum spacing and edge distances of Table 3.3: end distance e1 ≥ 1.2 d0, edge distance e2 ≥ 1.2 d0, pitch p1 ≥ 2.2 d0, gauge p2 ≥ 2.4 d0. The usual detailing values are 1.5 d0 for edges and 3 d0 for pitch, which also gives the full bearing factor αb.
  • Oversize and slotted holes reduce the slip resistance (ks = 0.85 to 0.63) and need a hardened washer.
  • National Annexes may change γM2 and γM3: check the one that applies to the project.

In the app

The Bolts tool of Steel Profiles lists M8 to M64 with the ISO 4014 head, ISO 4032 nut and ISO 7089 washer dimensions, the EN 14399 HV dimensions for M12 to M36 and the EN 1090-2 hole clearances, and computes shear, tension, punching shear, bearing (with your plate grade, thickness and end distance) and slip resistance for every class and surface.
