Livesick

Livesick is a music companion app built around live events and setlists, not a health app. It helps you discover what artists played during specific shows and quickly turn those songs into something you can listen to right away.

The core flow is simple: pick an event, browse the setlist, then add tracks to your Songs library or generate a playlist in Apple Music. This makes it easy to relive concerts and keep memorable performances in one place.

Livesick is designed for fans who follow tours, compare shows, and want a fast way to move from setlist discovery to playback without manual searching track by track.
