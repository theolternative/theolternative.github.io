Steel Profiles Free

Steel Profiles Free gives you a no-cost steel section reference with practical tools for quick checks on iPhone and iPad.

The app supports multiple standards and libraries, including AISC, CISC, European, and UK profiles. You can search sections by name or by geometric properties, then review the main values in a streamlined table and detail flow.

It is ideal for students and engineers who want broad profile coverage and fast lookup without carrying full manuals or complex desktop tools.
